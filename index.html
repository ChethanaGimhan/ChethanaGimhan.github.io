About

I’m a third-year Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, specializing in Cyber Security.

I build and deploy containerized DevOps pipelines, design databases, and work on database forensics. What pulls me toward security is the same habit that shapes my projects: understanding a system well enough to know where it fails. I’m especially interested in penetration testing, application security, and secure system design.

Away from the keyboard I’m a 400m hurdler and the vice-captain of the university’s men’s athletic team.
